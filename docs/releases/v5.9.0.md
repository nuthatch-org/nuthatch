Nuthatch 5.9.0 adds `nuthatch diff`: what happened in a block range, a block or a transaction, told in
fixed sentences from the rows a nest already holds (RFC-0071). It is read-only and makes no network
call beyond an optional receipt lookup for `--tx`. There is no storage migration, and no nest's NID,
data identity, registry hash or sealed segment changes.

## The story

`nuthatch diff --from A --to B` (or `--block`, or `--tx`) prints, section by section:

- **Control plane**, uncapped: every ownership, admin, role, pause and upgrade event in range.
- **Largest movements, per token**: the top transfers by amount and the top addresses by net flow.
  Amounts of two tokens are never compared, there are no prices, and every amount is exact decimal
  text scaled by `metadata.json`, never a float.
- **New holders**, and tables whose first row falls in the range.
- **Approvals**, with unlimited ones (2^256-1) named.
- **Flags and labels**: threshold flags raised in range, and every labelled address that appears.
- **Everything else**, told by `story` lines an author writes in `semantic.toml`, or grouped by each
  indexed parameter's most frequent values. Every row in range is counted in exactly one section.

A range above the sealed boundary is told and labelled provisional; `--final-only` refuses it.
`--since-last` runs on from the previous diff, keeping its cursor under `$XDG_STATE_HOME`, never in
the nest directory. Formats are `text`, `markdown` and `json`.

`semantic.toml` is outside the data identity, so adding a `story` line moves the NID but reuses the
nest's data.

## The flow

`nuthatch diff --flow` draws the token flow instead: one edge per (from, to, token) with the exact
amount, and each address's net flow, in text, JSON, DOT or Mermaid. WETH9's `Deposit` and
`Withdrawal` read as mint and burn for the listed wrapper on each chain only. Net flow is the sum of
the transfers seen, never a balance, and the output says what the nest cannot see.

## Three surfaces, one document

The CLI, `GET /diff` and the MCP `diff` tool build the same document and return byte-identical JSON.
The route takes one `/sql` permit and runs under `/sql`'s 30 s budget and hot-row ceilings; a bounded
mount refuses it. `nuthatch diff --url` asks the route, so story lines come from the instance's own
`semantic.toml`.

## Address-history nests

On an RFC-0063 address-history nest, the diff reads the watched addresses' transactions, internal
transfers and token transfers. A transaction's value and an internal transfer are movements of the
chain's own currency, kept apart from every ERC-20. For two Euler-exploit addresses, every native and
internal row matched Etherscan's `txlist` and `txlistinternal` to the wei.

## Limits

A diff reads at most 2,000,000 rows whole and draws at most 500,000 distinct (token, from, to) edges.
Past either it is refused with the reason, rather than leaving the per-cursor memory budget. The
widest real range measured had 119,056 edges, and two million GRT transfers peaked at 572 MiB.

## Compatibility and upgrade

Replace the binary and restart. A 5.8.2 store is used as it is.
