Nuthatch 5.11.1 stops a running publisher re-checking its whole mirror on every pass, and reads a
viem-style event ABI. There is no storage migration, and no nest's NID, registry hash or sealed
segment changes.

## A publisher checks each segment once

A nest started with `--publish-target` sent one HEAD per segment in the remote catalogue on every
pass: every `--publish-interval` (60 s by default) and on every seal. Segments are content-addressed
and never rewritten, so after the first answer these checks told it nothing, and their number grew
with history. On our own mirror they came to 4.6 million R2 reads a day, from two nests holding 5,418
and 1,388 segments, with no byte downloaded.

A running publisher now remembers, per dataset, the segments it has seen on the mirror or uploaded,
and checks only the rest. A restart costs one full check (#2401). `nuthatch publish sync` and
`nuthatch publish status` are unchanged.

One consequence: a segment deleted from the mirror by hand is not uploaded again until the publisher
restarts.

## An event ABI entry without `anonymous`

`init --abi` accepts an event entry that omits `anonymous`, as viem and other TypeScript ABIs do, and
reads it as not anonymous (#2397). Before, it refused the file.

## Compatibility and upgrade

Replace the binary and restart. A 5.11.0 store is used as it is.
